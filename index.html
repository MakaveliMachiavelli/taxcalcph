FAQ

Where's the crossover between 8% and graduated?

With OSD it's roughly ₱700k–₱1M gross, moving with your actual mix. Below it, graduated+OSD usually computes less; above it, 8%'s flat 8% usually wins. Run your number — that's what this page is for.

Does 8% have the ₱250k exemption too?

Yes — 8% applies to gross receipts in excess of ₱250,000.

What about VAT?

Separate decision: gross receipts above ₱3M generally require VAT registration (12%) regardless of income-tax option. This tool shows a warning when you cross ₱3M.

Is this official BIR software?

No — it's an independent calculator using the published R.A. 10963 rate tables. Always verify your final filing with your accountant or RDO.
